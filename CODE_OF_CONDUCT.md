# GARAM Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

GARAM: ZERO HOUR is a collaborative engineering project. We want participation to be respectful, constructive, technically rigorous, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors, players, reviewers, and maintainers with respect.
- Keep discussion professional and focused on the problem, evidence, and project impact.
- Explain technical decisions with reproducible observations where practical.
- Give and receive feedback in good faith; challenge ideas without attacking people.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish credentials, personal information, private messages, private repository content, or sensitive operational data without authorization.
- Credit original work and follow the license terms for third-party code, models, textures, audio, and other assets.
- Keep gameplay and security discussions responsible. Do not use project channels to coordinate harassment, cheating against other players, or attacks on systems.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, doxxing, personal attacks, sustained disruptive conduct, and publication of private information without consent are not acceptable. Deliberately abusive, deceptive, or malicious contributions may also be removed.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content, reject contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community and project.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository owner privately through the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/EldarJamiyev). Do not post private evidence or personal information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed with discretion and handled with attention to participant safety and privacy.

This policy applies in project repositories, issues, pull requests, code reviews, and other spaces used for GARAM project collaboration.
